Monthly fuel claim

Choose the billing month, then add or edit one claim per person. Enter the km, litres and the actual fuel bill and Gasoline is calculated for you (same formula as the Excel “Calculation” sheet); add tollgate, car wash, parking and the other expenses. Employees see the bill as soon as you save. Paid bills cannot be changed. Claims the employees submit themselves wait here until you approve them.

Payment advice

Choose a billing month, tick the people and print. Each document is one A4 sheet (two copies, top and bottom). The number is PC-year/ G<billing month>-<No.> (a September bill gives G9). After you pay, tick the people and press Mark as paid.

Set up authenticator app

1. Install Google Authenticator or Microsoft Authenticator on your phone.
2. Choose “Add account” → “Scan a QR code” and scan this code (or type the key below).
3. Enter the 6-digit code the app shows.

To remove the authenticator app, enter the code it currently shows.
